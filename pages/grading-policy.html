Grading policy

How the Class record turns raw scores into Prelim, Midterm, Semi-final and Final grades. Enter the college's approved categories, weights and transmutation table.

Colleges don't share one grading or transmutation formula, and some CHED program standards don't allow transmutation at all. Copy the table exactly from the college's approved grading policy; nothing here is filled in for you. The categories below are examples until you change them.

1. Assessment categories

0%

Quizzes, activities, projects, exams… Each period's initial grade = Σ (category's raw percentage × its weight). Weights must total 100%.
